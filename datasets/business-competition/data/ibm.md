# IBM Cloud - https://www.ibm.com/cloud
IBM Cloud is an enterprise hybrid-cloud platform for regulated, mission-critical, AI, and IBM Power/Z workloads.
## General Info
### Classification
- Hybrid Cloud Platform
### Short Description
- Enterprise cloud infrastructure and platform services for regulated and hybrid workloads.
### Description
- Provides infrastructure, Kubernetes, databases, storage, networking, watsonx, Red Hat OpenShift, and industry cloud services across public, private, and on-premises environments.
## Company Profile
### Category
- Enterprise Technology and Hybrid Cloud Vendor
### Regions
- Multizone regions and data centers across North America, South America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East.
### Customers
- Regulated enterprises, financial services, healthcare, government, telecom, and IBM Power/Z modernization customers.
### Partners
- Red Hat, SAP, VMware, NVIDIA, AMD, Infosys, Accenture, and IBM Business Partners.
## Market Positioning
### Segment
- Hybrid cloud, regulated workloads, enterprise AI, and mainframe/Power modernization.
### ICP
- Large organizations requiring compliance, data residency, hybrid deployment, or IBM-system continuity.
### Differentiators
- Red Hat OpenShift integration, IBM Power/Z support, regulated-industry offerings, confidential computing, and watsonx.
### PricingModel
- Consumption-based, service-specific plans, committed capacity, and enterprise agreements.
